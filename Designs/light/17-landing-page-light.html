Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about how Decay operates.

Will Decay close forms I am actively filling out? +
Never. Decay checks DOM activity states. Any tab with an active unsaved input field, playing media stream, or pinned status is permanently exempt from scheduled sweeps until submitted or closed manually.
How does active hour calculation work when my computer is asleep? +
Decay only counts seconds when you are actively using your computer. If you lock your screen, take lunch, or close your laptop for the weekend, the decay timer halts entirely. A 48-hour threshold equates to approximately 6 full working workdays.
Does Decay read or store the contents of my web pages? +
No. Decay only collects browser tab URLs, titles, and timestamps via local native APIs. It never inspects HTML bodies, cookies, session credentials, or keystrokes.
What happens if I need a tab back after weeks or months? +
Your archive is perpetual. Press ⌥⌘D (or Ctrl+K on Windows) anytime to search by URL keyword, page title, or date. Clicking restore reopens the tab in your preferred browser with one click.
