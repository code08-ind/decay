Clear Answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about how Decay operates.

help_outline Will Decay close forms I am actively filling out?

Never. Tabs with unsaved form input, audio output, or pinned states are protected by default through our built-in safety shields. Decay explicitly checks for dirty form state before queuing an archive.

help_outline How does active hour calculation work when my computer is asleep?

Decay calculates tab age using actual active user gaze. Time pauses automatically when your screen locks, during screensavers, or when video conferencing tools have active mic or camera streams.

help_outline Does Decay read or store the contents of my web pages?

Never. Decay only reads tab URLs and document titles locally. Page text, DOM nodes, cookies, and network payloads are never indexed, read, or transmitted anywhere.

help_outline What happens if I need a tab back after weeks or months?

All archived tabs remain permanently searchable in your local Library. Hitting ⌥⌘D allows you to summon tabs from days or months ago instantaneously, restoring scroll position whenever supported by the browser.
