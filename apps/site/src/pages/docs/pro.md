# Decay Pro

Decay is free. **Decay Pro** is a one-time purchase ($24, no subscription) that adds:

- Closing stale **Finder and Explorer folder windows** (they reopen from the archive).
- **Restoring a whole batch** at once, in its original order (⌘ Return).
- Keeping the archive **longer than 90 days**.
- **Unlimited custom timing rules** (three without Pro).
- **Batch names** made on your computer (Settings → Privacy & data): Apple's on-device model on Macs with Apple Intelligence, the words the tabs share elsewhere.

## Trial

Settings → Decay Pro → **Start free trial**: 14 days of Pro, once per computer, no account.

## Activating your license

Your license key is in your purchase email. Open the link in the email on a computer with Decay installed, or paste the key into Settings → Decay Pro and click **Activate**. The key works on all your computers and never expires; it's checked on your computer, never sent anywhere.

## When Pro ends

Nothing is deleted. Rules beyond the first three pause (they come back with Pro), and items archived from then on are kept for 90 days, while older ones keep the time they were archived with.
