# Refund Policy

**Effective:** 24 November 2026

You can try every Decay Pro feature free for 14 days before buying (Settings → Decay Pro → Start free trial).

If Decay Pro isn't right for you, you can get a **full refund within 14 days of purchase**, no questions asked. Refunds are handled by **Paddle.com**, our reseller and Merchant of Record: use the link in your receipt email, or contact Paddle at [paddle.net](https://paddle.net).

After a refund the license is no longer yours to use: deactivate it in Settings → Decay Pro. Decay itself keeps working, free, and nothing in your archive is deleted.
