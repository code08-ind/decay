# Decay beta — recruiting 20–30 testers

**Who:** developers and knowledge workers who keep 30+ tabs open (PRD §1.1). Aim for about 40 sign-ups to end with 20–30 active testers.

**Where:** your network first (colleagues, friends who are developers), then X / LinkedIn build-in-public posts, r/chrome_extensions and r/productivity (check each subreddit's self-promotion rules), and relevant Discord / Slack communities.

**Sign-up:** testers reply or DM with their email. You send each one the unlisted link and the [Tester Guide](./Tester%20Guide.md). The link stays private, so you know exactly who's in the beta.

## Post (X / LinkedIn)

```
I keep 60+ tabs open "just in case". So I'm building Decay: a browser extension that quietly archives the tabs you've stopped using, and brings any of them back with one shortcut.

• ages tabs by active browsing time, not the clock
• starts in shadow mode: nothing closes until you've seen what it would do
• Alt+Shift+D → type → Enter, and the tab is back where it was
• everything stays in your browser: no account, no server

Looking for 20–30 people with too many tabs to try it for 3–4 weeks (Chrome / Edge / Brave / Arc). Reply or DM me and I'll send the link.
```

## Short DM

```
Hey! I'm testing a small Chrome extension I built, Decay. It archives tabs you've stopped using and lets you search and restore them in a second. Everything stays local. It's a 3–4 week beta, about 15 minutes of your time in total. Want in? I'll send the link and a one-page guide.
```

## Tracking

Keep a simple sheet: name, email, invited on, installed (Y/N), day-7 check-in sent, automatic mode on, form submitted, call booked. Send two nudges: around day 8 ("time to review shadow mode") and 14 days after their automatic-mode date ("please send the form and metrics file"). Remind anyone who drops out to save the metrics file before uninstalling.
